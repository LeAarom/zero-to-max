# Zero to Max Guide

A RuneLite plugin that works out what to do next on any account, from a fresh account to maxed, and guides you there.

## What it does

- **Reads your account:** quests, achievement diaries and skill levels, all automatically. No setup.
- **Plans your route:** works through every quest for the Quest cape, every achievement diary, then every skill to 99. It always picks something you can do right now, or the quickest way to unlock the next thing.
- **GPS:** marks where to go. If you have the [Shortest Path](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/shortest-path) plugin installed, it draws a walking route there too.
- **Teleports:** suggests the best teleport you can use, including your POH (jewellery box, portals, spirit tree, fairy ring), max house worlds, and teleports you can buy on the Grand Exchange.
- **Training styles:** choose Fastest XP, Chill / AFK or Minigames, and switch between them with a hotkey (F12 by default).
- **Farm runs:** timers for herbs, trees, fruit trees, hardwood, calquat, spirit trees and birdhouses, a step-by-step GPS for each run, and a checklist of what to bring for your Farming level. Needs RuneLite's Time Tracking plugin turned on.
- **Slayer:** shows your current task and the best way to do it: melee, ranged, magic, cannon or bursting, plus where to go and what to bring.
